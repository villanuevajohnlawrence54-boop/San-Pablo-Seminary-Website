The Fourth Pillar

Pastoral Formation

To love and serve as Christ the Good Shepherd.

The Goal of the Journey

Pastoral formation is the culmination of the seminary journey. It is the practical application of spiritual, human, and intellectual growth, preparing the seminarian to become a true shepherd who knows his flock and is ready to lay down his life for them.

Parish Apostolate

Hands-on experience in parish life, assisting in liturgies, and learning the administration of a faith community.

Social Mission

Engaging with the poor, the sick, and the marginalized to witness the compassion of Christ in the world.

Catechesis

Learning the art of preaching and teaching the faith effectively to children, youth, and adults alike.
