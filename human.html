The Second Pillar

Human Formation

Developing the whole person in the image of God.

The Heart of the Development of the Whole Person

Human formation stands as the core and integrating foundation of all aspects of priestly formation. Through this dimension, the seminarian is gradually shaped into a person capable of deep, constant communion with God the Father, through His Son, Jesus Christ, in the Holy Spirit. It is precisely in human formation that one’s character, relationships, and interior disposition are refined, enabling a genuine and lived union with God that permeates every aspect of life and ministry.

Affective Maturity

Growth in emotional balance and self-awareness for compassionate relationships.

Moral Integrity

Consistent living of honesty, responsibility, and upright values.

Relational Capacity

Ability to form authentic, respectful, and service-oriented relationships.
